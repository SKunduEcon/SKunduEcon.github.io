About me.

Shiny Kundu

Economist · Macro Investor · Long-Term Strategist

Shiny Kundu is an independent economist and strategist based in New Delhi. Her experience spans J.P. Morgan Global Research, EY and CRISIL, alongside earlier policy research at the Reserve Bank of India's Department of Economic & Policy Research. Her work at the RBI examined India's balance-of-payments adjustment and monetary-policy transmission, and received the RBI Young Scholar Award.

Her interests lie at the intersection of macroeconomics, financial markets and public policy, with a particular focus on monetary and investment strategy, political economy, and the implications of AI for productivity and structural change. Her work brings together empirical research, long-horizon perspectives and scenario analysis to understand how shifting economic regimes translate into market outcomes and strategic decisions.

At J.P. Morgan, she was lead author of a study on long-term US equity returns, subsequently included in the University of Florida's graduate-level International Finance reading list.
