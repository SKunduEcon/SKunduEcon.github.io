About me.

Shiny Kundu

Economist · Macro Investor · Long-Term Strategist

An independent economist and strategist with more than 12 years of experience across macroeconomic research, financial markets and economic advisory. Interested in the intersections of economics, policy, markets and structural transformation.

← Back to homepage

Background

Economics, markets and the bigger picture.

My work has spanned long-term investment strategy and cross-asset research, foreign exchange and rates analysis, and corporate economic advisory. I am particularly interested in how macroeconomic shifts translate into investment decisions, business strategy and public policy.

Education

M.A. Economics, with a focus on Econometrics and Finance, Delhi School of Economics.

Areas of interest

Global macroeconomics, financial markets, India and emerging markets, political economy, AI and productivity, and structural change.
